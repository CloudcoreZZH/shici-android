## 共用约束

```text
Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a complete, polished high-fidelity design proposal board for the Chinese Android 16 offline English vocabulary app “拾词”. This is an implementable product UI design, not a marketing poster and not a physical phone photograph.
Canvas: a high-resolution PORTRAIT design sheet approximately 3:4. A narrow title strip at top, then EXACTLY SIX complete portrait mobile artboards in a precise THREE-COLUMN by TWO-ROW grid, then a small palette/type/component strip. Each artboard has realistic tall Android proportions, no physical device casing, no perspective, no iOS Dynamic Island. Thin Android status and gesture bars. Every screen fully inside canvas, no overlaps, adequate gutters. Use virtually all canvas space for readable UI, only a short design title and screen labels outside screens. Crisp legible Simplified Chinese, with large English typography and carefully aligned controls. Label six screens outside their top edges: “01 学习首页”, “02 查词详情”, “03 主动回忆”, “04 揭晓评分”, “05 复习安排”, “06 我的词书”.
Shared content and behavior across all styles:
Screen 01: “拾词”, a small date “9月18日 周五”, selected book “考研生词本”; a clear primary task, “待学习 12”, “到期复习 24”, strong “开始学习” button; a small 7-day schedule and quiet “今日已学 8”. Avoid repeating the same metric in many cards. Four bottom navigation items “查词 / 学习 / 词书 / 我的”, 学习 selected.
Screen 02: a back control and word “address”, phonetic “/əˈdres/”, sound icon; tabs “学习释义” and “全部释义”; Chinese definitions exactly “v. 处理；设法解决”, “n. 地址”, “v. 向…发表讲话”; a quiet note “编辑整理 · 非频率统计”; sticky bottom area with “已加入 3 次” and a clear button “再次加入 +1”. No numbered fake exam-frequency scores.
Screen 03: a QUIZ QUESTION with progress “3 / 10”, back and undo icons, very large word “address”, phonetic and sound icon, ample comfortable reading space. Prompt “回忆它的含义”. A thumb-reachable fixed bottom primary “显示答案”. IMPORTANT no Chinese definition or translation at all on this screen. No unrelated bottom tab bar while in focused learning.
Screen 04: same learning layout and word, now reveal “v. 处理；设法解决” and “n. 地址”, a short example “We need to address this problem.” with “我们需要处理这个问题。” and small note “原创例句”. A fixed rating area showing FOUR distinct tappable controls “忘记 1天”, “困难 1天”, “记得 2天”, “轻松 8天”. Make all four readable and comfortably sized, e.g. a balanced two-by-two arrangement or well-spaced four-cell dock. Top progress and undo. Revealing should not move the bottom action area off-screen.
Screen 05: “复习安排”, “今天到期 24”, “加入次数多的优先”; organized due list with words and chips “address · 加入 5 次”, “issue · 加入 3 次”, “approach · 加入 2 次”; a compact 7-day view; strong “开始复习”. Do not reveal their Chinese answers here and do not present future words as due. Bottom tab bar with 学习 selected.
Screen 06: “我的词书”, “考研生词本”, a restrained new-book plus icon, “128 词 · 待学 12 次”; search and clean rows “address” with “处理；设法解决” and “加入 5 次”, “issue” with “问题；议题” and “加入 3 次”, “approach” with “方法；接近” and “加入 2 次”, “maintain” with “维持；保养” and “加入 1 次”. Selected nav 词书. Counters must be unambiguous and readable.
Across all screens maintain one consistent design system: baseline grid, readable type, coherent icon strokes, obvious active states, strong contrast, generous 48dp-like touch areas, sustainable spacing. No social feed, no account, no ads, no subscriptions, no AI chatbot, no ranking, no decorative gaming badges, no unrelated phone widgets. Original visual identity; no copied third-party app branding. Do not add technical implementation labels such as FSRS/API/120 Hz inside normal learning UI. UI text should be concise and spelled correctly; numbers are illustrative. A complete visual system and distinct spatial composition matter more than decoration.

```

English word in a beautiful readable editorial serif; Chinese in a modern restrained sans serif. Asymmetric left-aligned typography, wide margins, subtle thin hairline dividers, compact small-cap-like section numbers.
